Settings

Changes save by themselves and apply from the next turn.

The story

Flavour and detail
How long each Dungeon Master step may be: low 100, medium 250, high 500 words at most.
Combat
How many fights come up. Medium is like a published adventure; high is mostly fighting.

Language and sound

Explain in Dutch
Keeps the full English text and adds a short, simple Dutch summary under it.
Narration audio
Read the story aloud on the screen. Some browsers also need one click on the play screen first.

Display (every screen)

Theme
Dark, light, or follow the computer.
Text size
How big the story text is, on the PC and on the TV.
Typing speed
How fast the story text appears.
Sound effects
Sounds triggered by the narration.
Roll on screen
Open the dice roller on the screen when the Dungeon Master calls for a roll (for games without phones).

Rules

Ruleset for new campaigns
Each campaign keeps the ruleset it was started with. This only chooses the default for new ones.

Characters

New gives simple choices and clear explanations. Experienced gives full options and short explanations.
